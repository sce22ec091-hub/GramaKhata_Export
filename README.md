# Grama-Khata 

Grama-Khata is a rural digital ledger application developed for small shopkeepers to manage customer credit transactions efficiently. The application replaces traditional paper-based “Vahi” books with a secure digital system for recording dues, tracking payments, sending reminders, and maintaining customer records through a simple Android application.

## Features

-  Add and manage customer details
-  Record credit and payment transactions
-  Track pending dues and payment history
-  Send payment reminders through SMS/WhatsApp
-  Secure and reliable digital storage
-  Simple and user-friendly Android interface
-  Replace traditional handwritten ledger books

##  Technologies Used

- **Android Studio**
- **Kotlin**
- **SQLite / Firebase**
- **XML for UI Design**

## Application Modules

### Customer Management
Store and manage customer information digitally.

### Credit Transactions
Record dues, payments, and balance amounts.

### Reminder System
Send reminders for pending payments.

### Transaction History
Maintain complete payment and credit records.

## Objectives

- Digitize rural credit management
- Reduce dependency on paper ledgers
- Improve transparency in transactions
- Help small shopkeepers manage dues efficiently
- Provide a simple and accessible mobile solution
